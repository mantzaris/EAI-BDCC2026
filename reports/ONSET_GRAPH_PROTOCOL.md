## Projection identities and missingness

For fully observed x, L is region membership and R its uniform regional average, so RL=I, z=Rx, u=(I−LR)x and Ru=0. For any fixed linear W, RWx=(RWL)z+RWu. The condition RWu=0 for every zero-mean u is **RW(I−LR)=0**, equivalently RW=QR. WL=LQ alone is not sufficient for a directed operator; it concerns a different invariant subspace. These are established linear-algebra facts, not new traffic dynamics or sufficient-statistic theorems. Tests will include a directed counterexample to the wrong implication and a small pair with identical regional means but different responses.

For missing history values S uses observed regional mean z_obs (zero if none), v=m*(x−Lz_obs), and the completed predictor state x_imp=Lz_obs+v. Then Rv=0 and Rx_imp=z_obs for fixed R/L, and the decomposition holds for **x_imp**. It is not an identity for unknown truth; it imputes predictor features only, never outcome labels. D/E/F instead normalize messages by available neighbors, a nonlinear mask-dependent operator. Their missing-data behavior is tested separately; the fixed-W projection claim is not transferred to them. Include poison-mask and complete-mask agreement checks.
