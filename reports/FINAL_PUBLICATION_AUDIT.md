It requests alternative text and disclosure of substantive LLM assistance. The draft has an anonymous author line, no identifying project URL, no invented affiliations, and an AI-assistance disclosure. No portal action occurred.

It names MathPhySci bibliographic formatting; this draft uses the unchanged conference-kit splncs04 numbered, alphabetically ordered style. The PDF and editable TeX/BibTeX are supplied. ## Primary-source comparison

| Wu et al., [Graph WaveNet, IJCAI2019/264](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2019/264), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.00121v1) | Adaptive node-embedding adjacency and dilated temporal convolutions are established. The verified source is the2023preprint; no unverified venue is invented. |
| Shao et al., [STID primary author PDF](https://zezhishao.github.io/assets/pdf/2022_CIKM_STID.pdf), [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05233) | Spatial/temporal identity plus simple MLPs is an established competitive baseline. Bibliography identifies the2024v2, with the source's accepted-TKDE note; publication volume/pages were not invented. |
| Liu et al., [LargeST primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08259) | Traffic-flow benchmark design is relevant context, but no LargeST data or experiment is part of this speed-event evaluation. No cross-dataset generalization is claimed. |
| Dwivedi et al., [Benchmarking Graph Neural Networks, JMLR24(43),2023](https://jmlr.org/papers/v24/22-0567.html) | Fair parameter budgets and reusable graph benchmarking are established. Approximately equal parameter counts here do not ensure equal function classes or optimization. |
| Schaub et al., [primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5381716/), [author-hosted version](https://scholar.harvard.edu/files/neaveoclery/files/1.4961065.pdf) | Equitable partitions, quotient operators and cluster synchronization require their stated assumptions. The fixed R/L identities in this manuscript are established algebra; directed mean closure is not implied by forward invariance alone. DOI10.1063/1.4961065. |
| Coifman and Maggioni, [Diffusion wavelets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acha.2006.04.004), [primary author-hosted PDF](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~saito/data/diffgeomharm/ACHA-Special/diffusionwavelets.pdf) | Multiscale operator compression is established. | Kim et al., [ResCAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05406) | Correcting traffic forecasts using previous errors and graph signals predates this study. Direct event-probability correction differs in target/interface but is not itself a novelty claim. Verified CIKM2022 DOI10.1145/3511808.3557432. |
