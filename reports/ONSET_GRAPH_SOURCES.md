# Focused primary-source and provenance update — 23 September 2026

This audit accompanies the executed [onset/graph study](ONSET_GRAPH_STUDY_REPORT.md). It does not replace that experiment or assert priority. Browser-rendered primary full texts were inspected; no paper bytes or hashes are invented. The [earlier audit](RESET_NOVELTY_AND_PROVENANCE.md) and [residual pilot discussion](RESIDUAL_PILOT_REPORT.md) retain their evidence and access limitations.

| Primary source / inspected sections | Established overlap, assumptions and difference from this pilot |
|---|---|
| Li et al., *Diffusion Convolutional Recurrent Neural Network: Data-Driven Traffic Forecasting*, [arXiv1707.01926v3 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.01926v3), §§2.2–2.3 and experiments | Bidirectional finite random-walk diffusion, recursively evaluated graph filters and recurrent multi-step traffic forecasting are established. The paper evaluates future-speed errors including PEMS-BAY. Our small additive event-probability correction with regional pooling is a controlled task comparison, not a replacement benchmark for DCRNN. Sparse multihop products alone add no novelty. |
| Wu et al., *Graph WaveNet for Deep Spatial-Temporal Graph Modeling*, [arXiv1906.00121v1 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.00121v1), §3 and experiments | Polynomial diffusion, learned node-embedding adjacency and temporal convolutions explicitly exploit latent dependencies. Its adjacency is optimized with the forecasting objective. F instead freezes an earlier-training residual-correlation graph; that difference does not establish a new learned-graph research area or causal road direction. No claim to beat Graph WaveNet is made. |
| Schaub et al., *Graph partitions and cluster synchronization in networks of oscillators*, [arXiv1608.04283v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1608.04283v2), §§II–III, equations2–6 | Equitable/external-equitable partitions, quotient operators and synchronized invariant subspaces have established theory. Their commuting orthogonal-projection derivation uses a symmetric graph Laplacian. A row-normalized directed W cannot inherit both forward invariance and mean closure without checking assumptions. Our counterexample to dropping those assumptions is elementary. No nonlinear future-event sufficiency result follows from a linear projection identity. |
| Coifman & Maggioni, *Diffusion wavelets* (2006), [DOI10.1016/j.acha.2006.04.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acha.2006.04.004), [primary full PDF](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~saito/data/diffgeomharm/ACHA-Special/diffusionwavelets.pdf), introduction and §2 | Multiscale graph diffusion, dyadic operator powers, coarse bases and operator compression are established. Locality and decreasing numerical ranks motivate efficient hierarchical constructions; symmetry is not essential to every extension. S uses fixed coordinate regions and three coarse/deviation feature streams, without constructing a diffusion-wavelet basis or proving low-rank compression. A multihop summary is not new merely because it is sent to a traffic predictor. |
| Yu et al., *Learning Coarse-Grained Dynamics on Graph*, [arXiv2405.09324v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.09324v2), projection/Mori–Zwanzig formulation and graph-reach analysis | The paper addresses learned coarse dynamics, projected memory and multihop graph architecture under specified smooth dynamical-system assumptions. S is an explicit current/history representation, not a proved closure or learned Mori–Zwanzig memory model. Our nonlinear binary event and fixed summaries require separate predictive evidence; this pilot does not establish a new closure theorem. |

The closest residual/acquisition antecedents remain [ResCAL, arXiv2209.05406](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05406), [MURECAST, DOI10.3390/sym18091485](https://doi.org/10.3390/sym18091485) and [EDDI, PMLRv97/ma19c](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/ma19c.html). The repository's prior audit covers residual traffic correction, selective correction and adaptive information acquisition. MURECAST's full publisher text remained inaccessible in that audit (HTTP429); its indexed publisher abstract does not support a completed formula-level comparison. No new access claim is made here. No optional acquisition experiment ran, so there is no acquisition contribution to distinguish from EDDI.

The standard clipped squared-loss identity and R/L decomposition are established algebra. A potential substantive contribution would need evidence that a specified affordable representation captures useful event information beyond equally informed aggregate updating and matched topology nulls, plus a defensible deployment/evaluation setting. This study does not establish that. A different event, a graph layer, GPU execution, familiar conditional-variance identity or failed experiment alone does not create publishable novelty.

Public availability is established; exact analysis/redistribution rights and upstream preprocessing remain unresolved. No retrieved evidence establishes a definite prohibition that was bypassed; neither does a mirror or software license establish permission. Unknown upstream imputation may affect both outcome support and how much local/graph signal is physical rather than preprocessing. No account, third-party contact or access-control bypass occurred.
