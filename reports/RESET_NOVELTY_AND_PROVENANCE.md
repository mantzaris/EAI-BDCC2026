# Restart source and measurement audit — 23 September 2026

This is a focused primary-source comparison for the two candidates in [RESEARCH_RESET.md](RESEARCH_RESET.md), not an exhaustive priority search or a legal opinion. Approximately three closest works per candidate were inspected at full-text level, with a few directly relevant background checks. The linked source record distinguishes full text, abstract-only access and failed access. No primary source establishes the novelty of a proposed restart.

## Candidate 1: aggregation sufficiency and task observation

| Primary source and inspected material | Relevant result and assumptions | Exact overlap and proposed difference |
|---|---|---|
| Geiger & Temmel, *Lumpings of Markov chains, entropy rate preservation, and higher-order lumpability* (2014), [DOI 10.1239/jap/1421763331](https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1421763331); [primary full text](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/AE22AA331F3FBE1361498E30E7A37160/S0021900200120807a.pdf/div-class-title-lumpings-of-markov-chains-entropy-rate-preservation-and-higher-order-lumpability-div.pdf). Definitions and lumpability/entropy sections inspected. | Finite irreducible aperiodic time-homogeneous Markov chain and deterministic projection. Strong higher-order lumpability concerns a projected Markov property for every initial law; weaker stationary properties and entropy-rate preservation differ. | Aggregation preserving dynamical information is established. A future sensor-count event must additionally be measurable from retained information. The traffic graph weight matrix is not the transition matrix on traffic configurations. A useful finite-history event-risk bound would be a different result, but none is supplied here. |
| Schaub et al., *Graph partitions and cluster synchronization in networks of oscillators* (2016), [author full text, arXiv:1608.04283v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1608.04283v2). Sections II and III on linear and nonlinear dynamics inspected. | External equitable partitions of graph Laplacians yield exact quotient behavior for linear cell means and invariant synchronized subspaces. For nonlinear node dynamics, averaging generally does not commute with the dynamics; manifold invariance is not unrestricted mean closure. | Zero structural defect and quotient reduction have substantial antecedents. Neither preserves every nonlinear event functional or handles arbitrary off-manifold within-block states. The new two-node example clarifies this limitation but is elementary, not a new graph-abstraction theorem. |
| Montanari, Duan, Aguirre & Motter, *Functional observability and target state estimation in large-scale networks*, PNAS 119(1), e2113750119 (2022; online 2021), [DOI 10.1073/pnas.2113750119](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2113750119), [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8740740/). Results, rank conditions and sensor-placement assumptions inspected. | Linear dynamics, known input and linear target Fx; functional observability requires row(F) within the observability row space. Structural results concern sparsity/generic parameters, with coordinate sensors/targets; placement algorithms impose additional graph conditions. | Estimating only a task-relevant target with fewer sensors and less observer computation is already explicit. A discontinuous future event probability under noisy, partly observed traffic differs from linear target-state reconstruction. No valid extension is established by substituting “digital twin.” Sensor placement is not proved exactly optimal merely because a minimal-set problem is formulated. |

A key statistical boundary comes from Shah & Peters, *The Hardness of Conditional Independence Testing and the Generalised Covariance Measure*, [DOI 10.1214/19-AOS1857](https://doi.org/10.1214/19-AOS1857), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.07203). Theorem 2 and Remark 4 show the difficulty of uniform conditional-independence testing over unrestricted IID law classes with continuous conditioning; the remark includes discrete tested variables. Their practical tests require regression-rate and other assumptions. This does not directly analyze dependent traffic time series, but it precludes treating unrestricted sufficiency testing as a routine consequence of a non-significant classifier comparison. Restricting the process class and proving appropriate statistical validity are obligations, not accomplished results.

Two other observability sources delimit scope. Whalen et al., [*Observability and Controllability of Nonlinear Networks: Role of Symmetry*](https://math.gmu.edu/~tsauer/pre/PRX5.pdf), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevX.5.011005, was inspected for linear/nonlinear observability and conditioning: dynamics and the measurement function matter beyond graph topology. O'Clery et al., DOI [10.1103/PhysRevE.88.042805](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.88.042805), directly connects consensus coarse graining with external equitable partitions and observability. Its publisher abstract was accessible; the publisher PDF returned unauthorized access. No uninspected theorem from that paper is treated as checked, and the exact comparison remains incomplete.

## Candidate 2: budgeted acquisition and task communication

| Primary source and inspected material | Relevant result and assumptions | Exact overlap and proposed difference |
|---|---|---|
| Krause, Singh & Guestrin, *Near-optimal Sensor Placements in Gaussian Processes*, JMLR 9:235–284 (2008), [primary full text](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume9/krause08a/krause08a.pdf). Section 4, submodularity and approximation qualifications inspected. | Gaussian-process field information objective. MI between a chosen set and its complement is submodular but not globally monotone; approximate monotonicity and the stated greedy approximation require additional kernel/noise/discretization conditions. | Information-based sensor selection and cost-aware approximations are established. Causal traffic polling needs its own justified objective and dependence assumptions. |
| Courtade & Weissman, *Multiterminal Source Coding under Logarithmic Loss*, IEEE TIT 60(1):740–761 (2014), [DOI 10.1109/TIT.2013.2288257](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2013.2288257), [author full text](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~courtade/pdfs/CourtadeWeissman_TransITJan2014.pdf). Asymptotic rate/distortion results use their specified distributed observation structure. | Rates for communicating information relevant to a probabilistic target are established. Finite causal packets, dependent traffic histories, read costs and Brier distortion differ. Those differences require analysis; they do not automatically confer novelty. A true-posterior message is a strong simple baseline. |
| Shao, Mao & Zhang, *Task-Oriented Communication for Multi-Device Cooperative Edge Inference*, [DOI 10.1109/TWC.2022.3191118](https://doi.org/10.1109/TWC.2022.3191118), [author full text, arXiv:2109.00172v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.00172v3). System model, Proposition 1, Remarks 1–2 and proposed methods inspected. | Devices extract/encode task information; information-bottleneck formulations, finite communication and selective retransmission are explicit. Ideal minimal-sufficiency/conditional-independence assumptions are distinct from what variational training guarantees. Experiments concern cooperative inference, including image information. | Task-aware compression and selective communication across edge devices already exist. A fixed traffic event alone is not a contribution. Any proposed causal acquisition policy must distinguish unobserved sensor reads from compression of already observed features and beat practical schedules/codecs under real budgets. |

## Traffic forecasting and the failed method's antecedents

Geroliminis & Sun, [*Properties of a well-defined macroscopic fundamental diagram for urban traffic*](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/911cd085-6083-4c01-b8ba-ca2ae231910c/content), DOI 10.1016/j.trb.2010.11.004, connects macroscopic behavior with spatial distribution and correlations in traffic. Its flow/occupancy and urban/freeway setting differs from this speed-risk forecast, but aggregation heterogeneity is longstanding motivation, not a new discovery. The full repository copy was accessible after an old bitstream URL failed.

The originating [DCRNN project](https://github.com/liyaguang/DCRNN) and [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01926) already formulate graph-based traffic forecasting. The closeout does not benchmark or claim to improve DCRNN; its model and aggregation statements cannot substitute for a competitive predictive comparison. New architecture development is outside scope.

The preserved [Stage 1 proof audit](STAGE1_PROOF_AUDIT.md) and [Stage 2 novelty audit](STAGE2_DATA_AND_NOVELTY.md) identify selective failure-probability refinement (Elfverson et al., [arXiv:1408.6856](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.6856)), multilevel selective refinement in subset simulation ([arXiv:2208.05392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.05392)), and mixed-monotone box abstractions (Coogan–Arcak, 2015). Their overlap is preserved, not displaced by a restart narrative. The existing exact D-based enclosure may have a specific formulation, but distinctness and usefulness remain unresolved. Historical partial access to the Couthures and crowd-risk full texts is not upgraded to completed full-text comparison here. Neither a standard conditional-variance identity nor the new elementary counterexamples repair those novelty gaps.

**Current finding:** public availability is established; code licensing is established; exact measurement lineage, applicable analysis terms and redistribution rights are not positively established. No definite prohibition has been identified in the retrieved evidence either. The accurate status is unresolved, not “public means permitted” and not “research is known to be forbidden.” In particular, unknown upstream imputation could affect a future no-leakage claim even though the repository's own transformations and splits are chronological.

Raw files remain private and excluded from Git. No new measurements or dataset were downloaded. The [new source manifest](../manifests/closeout/source_audit.json) records primary URLs, DOIs, access date, sections/evidence level and failures. Browser-rendered source text was inspected but no archival source bytes were added; absent content hashes/byte counts are not invented. Original source-evidence hashes and all historical provenance records remain unchanged. No external message, registration or permission request was sent.

These unresolved matters reinforce deferral, but are not its sole reason: even a resolved analysis license would leave contribution, deployment and independent event-support obligations unmet.
