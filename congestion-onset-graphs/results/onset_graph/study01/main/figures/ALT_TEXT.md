# Figure descriptions

- **endpoint_effects**: Four paired contrasts for onset and all-time Brier. C is worse than B; E is better than D on onset but not B. Gray intervals show the four-contrast multiplicity adjustment. All validation was exposed.
- **alarm_calibration**: Raw and calibrated Brier, per-seed frozen recall/FPR, and first-seed calibration bins. Recalibration worsens onset scores; E seed01 exceeds 7% false alarms. Bin support varies.
- **topology_controls**: D/E are indistinguishable from the first trained rewired null on onset, although they beat the second. Self/internal controls do not establish useful actual topology.
- **temporal_robustness**: Fixed validation blocks and all chronological development evaluations. Local and graph effects vary by time; no period was selected for favorable results.
- **illustrative_window**: Timestamp-selected Apr20 08:00 window in region0. Regional mean and graph-deviation summaries accompany four local categorical histories. It is neither raw speed redistribution nor population-level evidence.

All figures have PDF/SVG versions and PNG previews. CPU work rendered saved CUDA outputs; no CPU fitting, scoring or bootstrap was performed.
