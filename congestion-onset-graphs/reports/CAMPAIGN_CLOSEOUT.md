# Campaign closeout — 23 September 2026

The current method campaign is closed. Its prespecified predictive and computational gates failed. The narrow supported finding is that **this fitted reconstruction/graph-simulation pipeline was inadequate relative to a simple aggregate-plus-heterogeneity classifier, and its selective enclosures increased complete runtime on the measured workload**. The matched classifier experiment did not establish a useful benefit from adding sensor identities. It did not establish that those identities contain no predictive information.

The separate [restart assessment](RESEARCH_RESET.md) recommends **C: defer a restart because neither assessed candidate currently has a defensible contribution and evaluation path**. No Stage 3 or restart pilot was executed or scheduled.

## Diagnosis retained from the campaign

The single allowed model repair addressed observed residual location/drift, using calendar and current operating regime with complete multivariate residual sequences. Its inner Brier improved from 0.04771 to 0.03537, but it failed the outer adequacy gate. No second speculative repair was attempted. Hidden-state conditional coverage also fell short of nominal 95% (about 76.8% and 83.1% in the inner 0%/15% cases), despite retained-constraint errors near 1e-11. Full observation removes uncertainty about retained current values, not future dynamics or forcing error. Inverse-transform consistency checks did not identify a clipping implementation bug. Matched 30-minute speed MAE was 2.66293 original, 2.48514 repaired and 2.26683 persistence. Calibration changes risk scores without validating trajectories.

For raw independent fine Monte Carlo with fixed conditional model and N scenarios, expected excess Brier due to simulation is p(1−p)/N ≤ 1/(4N), conditional on the origin and outcome. Averaging origins preserves this bound. N=1,024 gives 0.000244140625; saved estimated contributions are approximately 0.00002460–0.00003411. At N=256 the worst-case bound is 0.0009765625. These scales do not make increasing sample count a credible default remedy for the original 0.03680–0.05785 gaps. This argument does not bound an observed score difference, guarantee the realized effect of increasing N, or apply unchanged to calibrated, clipped, dependent or two-level estimates. See [saved MC accounting](../results/stage2/mc_contribution.csv).

The enclosure failure is valid but uninformative error propagation, not a discovered recurrence violation. At full retention, saved step-six radius contributions were initial variation 17.036, structural defect 19.372 and forcing 21.665, total 58.073 against actual deviation about 1.105. The original Wbar+D row sums 2.824–4.985 amplify bounds despite unit fine-graph row sums. The row-wise tightening retains cancellation and removes unnecessary amplification but still resolves none of 12,288 coupled scenarios (16 origins × three retention levels × 256). Oracle fine block minima/maxima reduce unresolved fractions, but obtaining them requires fine trajectories and is not a deployable improvement. Omitting a nonzero radius term is not a certificate.

Complete CPU preparation/refinement timings include initial reduction, forcing, reconstruction, compaction and fine work. The tightened path costs 22–26% more than fine in five repeats. Stage 1's measured hybrid GPU pipeline likewise did not save runtime. Historical classifier batch prediction time excludes feature construction and must not be advertised as an end-to-end latency comparison. The [width/cost figure](../results/stage2/figures/enclosure_width_cost.png) and [paired prediction figure](../results/stage2/figures/paired_predictions.png) are preserved, not regenerated.

## Scientific value and closeout limits

This package is a reproducible bounded diagnosis of one failed method on one exposed task. It documents a useful practical distinction between exact event enclosures and useful event enclosures, and between predictive model quality and sampling error. The theoretical components overlap established selective simulation, graph abstraction and conditional estimation work; the [updated source audit](RESET_NOVELTY_AND_PROVENANCE.md) identifies that overlap. The specific D construction's distinctness remains unresolved. No first-of-its-kind claim survives this review.

The negative result is **not currently substantial enough for a standalone research contribution**. It lacks independent confirmation, broad enough failure characterization to support a general negative claim, and a demonstrated novel impossibility or computational result. The exact counterexamples below clarify assumptions but are elementary and do not provide that missing contribution. Preserve this as a technical research record; do not draft a submission around failed gates alone.
