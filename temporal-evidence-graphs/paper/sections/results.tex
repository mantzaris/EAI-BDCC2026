\subsection{Storage Structure and Semantic Structure Differ}

The read-only export covers \SemExportScopes{} retained Neo4j scopes; counts
match the original ledger and \SemVerifiedPaths{} representative stored paths
are verified. M1 alone stores \SemStorageNodes{} nodes and \SemStorageEdges{}
relations across its replay scopes. These are storage totals, not independent
observations. Membership and extraction rules contribute much of the edge mass
through their construction rules. Neither those edges nor the accepted-citation mirror
demonstrate additional reasoning depth.

The query core averages \SemCoreSizeMin--\SemCoreSizeMax{} nodes across sources,
or \SemCoreRatioMin--\SemCoreRatioMax\% of visible storage. This reduction follows
the fixed selection of answer propositions and declared inputs; the active graph
also includes other visible evidence. Observation witnesses remain recoverable
through sidecars. The full stored totals count historical versions, whereas core sizes count
visible propositions per query; subject-level aggregation prevents replay copies
from becoming independent samples.

Only \SemParentClaims{} of \SemDisplayedClaims{} displayed M1 claims declare a
parent: \SemParentlessPercent\% are parentless. Claim-parent depth never exceeds
one in the displayed waveform cores. Figure~\ref{fig:instances} illustrates why
even that parent edge need not create transitive exposure: the comparison cites
the revised target feature directly. Across \SemRevisionBatches{} revision
batches, \SemEligibleBatches{} require a withdrawal and \SemNonzeroExposure{}
have nonzero exposure; the locality audit finds \SemLocalityViolations{} violations.
This structure explains the completed direct/full equivalence without attributing
it to an absence of stored paths or to a particular database engine.

The contract crosswalk sharpens this interpretation. Of \SemContractInvalid{}

The extension completes \SemCoreCases{} common candidates with \SemCoreCalls{}
GPU calls, including \SemCoreRepairs{} repairs, in \SemCoreMinutes{} minutes.
Its \SemReplayCells{} method/update replays include exact and generated structures.
Neo4j and the relational control agree in all \SemParityGroups{} matched
origin/update groups. Direct maintenance misses \SemFixtureMissed{} of
\SemFixtureRequired{} required changes in exact programs and
\SemGeneratedMissed{} of \SemGeneratedRequired{} in admitted generated programs;
full maintenance misses none and causes no collateral withdrawal.
The predicted missed set agrees in all \SemPredictionAgreement{} of
\SemPredictionGroups{} eligible groups (Figure~\ref{fig:structure}).

Realized structure differs from the requested structure. Admitted generated
answers have depth one in \SemRootOne{} cases and depth two in \SemRootTwo{};
\SemMissingRoots{} cases have no admitted answer root. None reaches depth three
or four. The model commonly groups tests into route claims followed by an answer,
without expressing the requested longer chain. Exact programs consequently
test the deeper counterexamples, while generated programs test the dependencies
the model actually expressed. Under primary-route loss, all
\SemFixtureAlternative{} exact and \SemGeneratedAlternative{} generated
two-route answers with admitted roots retain support; single-route roots do not.
Figure~\ref{fig:topology} makes preservation inspectable: the numerical update
changes A1 from \ViewRouteOld{} to \ViewRouteNew{} $\mu$S, below its
\ViewRouteThreshold{} $\mu$S threshold. CA is withdrawn, but CB and the answer
remain displayed. Direct and full maintenance agree for this route-loss update;
necessary-change conditions in the population test expose excluded dependents.
Necessary intermediate dependencies and surviving alternatives, rather than path
length alone, determine maintenance behavior. In \SemSameSize{} paired cases,
programs with the same evidence and baseline record count have different exposure,
showing why total storage size is insufficient.

An admission-table omission was discovered after the core completed: primitive
test names were defined in the input but unavailable as claim proposition names.
It generated \SemUnknownPrimitive{} unknown-proposition rejection reasons.
A separate corrected replication normalizes those names to their supplied
comparators without changing candidates, witnesses or links. It admits
\SemAliasRoots{} roots and repeats \SemAliasCells{} CPU database cells:
direct maintenance misses \SemAliasMissed{} of \SemAliasRequired{} required
changes, while full maintenance again misses none. The original extension
scores above remain frozen. Independent truth-table scoring distinguishes
initially true statements from sound, complete witness families, so the admission
correction is not treated as new model reasoning.

\subsection{Operational Cost and Adequate Replacement}

The original systems workload produces \SystemsAdequate{} adequate replacements
among \SystemsExplanations{} completed candidates. All omit the required
preceding-window difference. Their successful-replacement times therefore remain
unresolved through observation. At the highest offered rate, both databases accumulate
backlog and delayed flags. The extension isolates maintenance of a common answer;
it adds no post-correction generation and hence establishes selective withdrawal
and preservation, not automatic completion of a new adequate answer. Structural
replay timings include diagnostic traversal and whole-program evaluation; they are instrumented costs, not production latency estimates.
