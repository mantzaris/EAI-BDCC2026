\section{Evaluation Design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Preserved Three-Source Study}

The unchanged \texttt{minimum\_v1} study uses synthetic physiological-style
waveforms, WESAD laboratory recordings~\cite{schmidt2018,wesad}, and PPG-DaLiA
daily-activity recordings~\cite{reiss2019,ppgdalia}. Each contributes ten held-out
subjects with two episodes each. Real-source splits use PCG64 seed 20260928 and
five separate development subjects. Physiological/activity labels enter neither
generation nor checking; outcomes concern recorded features.

Each episode has a 30-second target and a preceding 120-second comparison
interval. Four replay variants provide clean/benign, channel-fault, delayed,
and numerical-correction events; three knowledge checkpoints occur at target
end plus 1, 31 and 91 seconds. Four question families require the target EDA
median plus either its preceding-window difference, EDA missing fraction, or
acceleration variability. These requirements are fixed independently of answers.
Five methods produce 3,600 initial cases. B0 displays unchecked proposals;
B1 performs direct maintenance over indexed records; B2 uses Neo4j with direct
maintenance; M1 uses Neo4j with transitive maintenance; B3 uses indexed SQLite
and recursive CTEs with the same transitive support semantics. All checked
methods share fresh-answer checks and at most one repair attempt.

Pinned Qwen3-8B BF16~\cite{qwen3} originally uses a three-claim contract and
1,536 output tokens. Both studies use the existing RTX 5090, verified CUDA
parameters and forward tensors, and vLLM structured decoding~\cite{vllm}.
Database/statistical work uses the host CPU; Neo4j Community 5.26 and indexed
SQLite retain their adapters. The three original B0 transport failures remain
empty outputs.

\subsection{Independent Semantic Evaluation}

The new \texttt{semantic\_analysis\_v1} oracle resolves values and witnesses
from immutable events and task specifications, never support flags or a neural
verifier. Canonical identity includes dataset, subject/session, asserted interval,
quantity, operation, normalized value/unit, temporal mode and logical version.
Correctly labelled background can be grounded without meeting a target
requirement. Wrong numbers, subjects and target attributions still fail.
Citation/witness fidelity is separate from directly checkable proposition truth.

For emitted checkable propositions $C(q,t)$ and answerable required propositions
$R(q,t)$, we report
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 P_{\rm grounded}&=\frac{\sum_{c\in C}\mathbf 1[\text{source-grounded }c]}{|C|},\\
 R_{\rm required}&=\frac{|\{r\in R:\text{matched by a grounded }c\}|}{|R|}.
\end{aligned}
 \label{eq:semanticmetrics}
\end{equation}
Every requirement is matched once, so repeated paraphrases cannot increase
recall. The optional harmonic summary $S=2P_{\rm grounded}R_{\rm required}/
(P_{\rm grounded}+R_{\rm required})$ combines grounding and task completeness;
their universes differ, so it is not conventional set F1. Empty output has
precision NA and zero recall/summary when the question is answerable. Fully
unanswerable questions have recall/summary NA and separately checked absence
statements. Partially answerable questions contribute their answerable facts.

We separately score raw and final proposals, accepted claims and displays.
Raw text, literal discrepancies and additional interpretation terms remain in a
limited prose audit; structured scores do not certify unrestricted language.
Temporal integrity reuses each prior answer before arrival, after arrival and
after maintenance. These are discrete checkpoints, not recovery curves.
Withdrawal and adequate replacement are separate outcomes.

\subsection{Controlled Structural Extension}

\texttt{structure\_study\_v1} selects the first five naturally sorted existing
held-out subjects per source and both episodes, before observing new outputs.
The 30 episodes reuse participants; they are not a new independent cohort.
Four intended evidence-to-answer depths (1--4) and two support regimes yield
240 cases. Each route applies four explicit numerical comparisons to EDA median,
EDA IQR, acceleration SD and EDA missing fraction. Bounds are fixed from the
clean episode so the routes initially satisfy the tests. The answer states
whether at least one eligible window meets all four criteria. A second route
uses the disjoint preceding window, providing a genuinely different observation
witness for this existential statement. The task makes no physiological diagnosis.

Depth changes grouping while preserving the tests: direct inputs; a complete
route then answer; a two-test prefix then route and answer; or first-test,
two-test prefix, route and answer. Exact compiled fixtures instantiate these
programs. Separately, the model proposes up to 16 claims and explicit witness
lists, with a 4,096-token output allowance and at most one repair. Model links
are never filled in by the compiler. Requested and realized depths, omitted
roots and incomplete alternative-witness families are retained. Two development
pilots precede the core freeze; the Supplement records the prompt clarification.

One common admitted candidate per case is replayed through all maintenance
methods, with no post-correction generation. B0 here is a maintenance-off
counterfactual on that same admitted content. Four independent update conditions
make the first test false in every route, remove only the primary route, make a
benign numerical change, or revise an unused cardiac feature. Neo4j and relational
methods receive identical candidates, events and predicates. An independent
truth-table evaluator enumerates the bounded Boolean assignments to test witness
entailment and equivalence, allowing different valid derivations. A separate
event-log evaluator determines the actual required changes.

\subsection{Graph Measurement and Uncertainty}

Read-only Neo4j exports preserve nodes, relations, assessment times, Cypher and
element IDs. Counts match the original ledger and sampled stored paths. We
measure full storage, active graphs and query cores separately: typed mixing,
degree/depth, direct citations, witness counts, reach and actual changes.
Dependency degree excludes SUPPORTS and provenance mirrors; meaningful zero
categories remain. No power-law or embedding interpretation is imposed.

Snapshots and variants are first aggregated within episodes, then episodes within
subjects. Pointwise 95\% intervals use 2,000 subject-cluster bootstrap resamples
with seed 20260929. Historical versions and replay copies are never independent
participants. The Supplement provides denominators, stage-specific distributions,
all original contract results, and extended operational accounting.
