## Corrections and selection

For fixed baseline probability p, predict `clip(p + alpha*r_theta(Z), 0, 1)`. Use ordinary mean Brier loss plus `lambda*mean(r_theta^2)` and AdamW parameter decay 1e-4. Train with alpha=1. Initialize only the output head to zero; internal representations and the additive path remain active. Check that the head obtains a nonzero gradient and subsequent steps update internal parameters.

B uses aggregate features and p only. C adds a shared ordered-history temporal encoder per sensor. D has the same encoder/parameter count and pooling, with distance-similarity neighbor messages replacing C's self messages. Both preserve the same 16-region identity through masked pooling. Local channels are scaled state, deviation from its available block mean, one-step change and valid mask; no future values or retrospective error-defined feature enters. Inputs and messages are masked before use, with zero contribution from fully unavailable sensors. C and D have equal parameter counts, but the self-message and graph-message branches have different effective function classes and arithmetic costs.

Widths: temporal 16, aggregate 32, final hidden 32; residual output is `0.5*tanh(head)`. No architecture search. Per family compare exactly lambda in {0.01,0.1}, using seed 20260923, AdamW learning rate .001, batch 256, at most 60 epochs, early-stop patience 8, minimum improvement 1e-6. Fit correction origins at stride 3; early stopping uses the complete eligible role. Choose the setting by its best early-stop Brier, ties favor stronger regularization. Then run that setting independently with seeds 20260924, 20260925 and 20260926, keeping every attempt and checkpoint. No best-seed selection and no unannounced ensemble.

Select one scalar alpha per fitted replicate from {0,.25,.5,1} on the shrinkage block, ties favor zero/smaller alpha. Uncertainty: 2,000 paired resamples of whole days and noncircular moving three-day blocks, GPU seed 20260927; preserve all overlapping origins and all models/seeds within each selected day. Report seed variation separately from time-block uncertainty. The provisional useful point target is at least 5% Brier reduction over A_T; a local claim also requires improvement over B; a graph claim needs evidence beyond C. Promising means a useful point improvement with supported paired direction and acceptable detection; wide intervals or weak support are inconclusive, and a clear missed gate is negative. These are exploratory pilot classifications, not a significance theorem.

For every fitted correction and transferred diagnostic, verify on GPU, after clipping, `mean(base_loss-new_loss) = 2*mean(delta*(Y-p_base))-mean(delta^2)`, with FP64 summaries and tolerance 1e-10. Alignment and magnitude are reported separately. This is an established squared-loss identity, not new theory.
